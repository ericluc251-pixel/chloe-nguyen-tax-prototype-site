Questions from Katy clients

Which documents do I need for an ITIN?

A valid passport is enough on its own. Without one, you need a combination of other current documents listed in the Form W-7 instructions, such as a national ID card and a birth certificate. Documents must be originals or copies certified by the agency that issued them.

When does an ITIN expire?

An ITIN expires if it is not used on a federal tax return for three tax years in a row. It expires on December 31 of the third year.
