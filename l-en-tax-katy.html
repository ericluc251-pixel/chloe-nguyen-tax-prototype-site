Tax preparer in Katy, Texas

Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory prepares and e-files federal tax returns for individuals, families and self-employed people in Katy. Texas has no state personal income tax, so most Katy residents file only a federal return, and we check whether you lived or worked in another state during the year. You can start online, upload photos of your forms and sign from your phone.

What’s included

  • Form 1040 for W-2 workers, families and retirees
  • Schedule C for gig, salon and other self-employed income
  • Credits checked on every return, such as the child tax credit and education credits
  • A second preparer reviews each return before it is e-filed

Serving Katy

Katy sits along Interstate 10 west of Houston and stretches across parts of Harris, Fort Bend and Waller counties.
