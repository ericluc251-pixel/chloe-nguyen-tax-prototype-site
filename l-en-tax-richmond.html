Tax preparation · Richmond, TX

Tax preparer in Richmond, Texas

Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory prepares and e-files federal tax returns for individuals, families and self-employed people in Richmond. Texas has no state personal income tax, so most Richmond residents file only a federal return, and we check whether you lived or worked in another state during the year. You can start online, upload photos of your forms and sign from your phone.

What’s included

  • Form 1040 for W-2 workers, families and retirees
  • Schedule C for gig, salon and other self-employed income
  • Credits checked on every return, such as the child tax credit and education credits
  • A second preparer reviews each return before it is e-filed

Serving Richmond

Richmond is the county seat of Fort Bend County and sits on the Brazos River. We help Richmond families, retirees and self-employed workers with federal returns, ITIN applications and quarterly estimated payments, and Richmond businesses with monthly bookkeeping and payroll filings. Our Katy office is within driving distance for in-person appointments.
