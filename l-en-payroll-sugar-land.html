Questions from Sugar Land clients

When is Form 941 due?

The last day of the month after each quarter: April 30, July 31, October 31 and January 31.

Do Texas employers withhold state income tax?

No. Texas has no state income tax to withhold, but most employers pay state unemployment tax through the Texas Workforce Commission.
