What to bring for your 2026 tax return (filing in 2027)

A checklist of the documents to gather for your 2026 federal return, so your appointment is quick and nothing is missed.

By Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory · Published October 6, 2026 · Tax year 2026

Most tax forms arrive in January and early February 2027. Start a folder now and add each one as it comes.

For everyone

  • A photo ID for you and your spouse
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return
  • Last year’s return, if we did not prepare it
  • Your bank routing and account numbers for a direct-deposit refund
  • Your Identity Protection PIN letter, if the IRS sent you one

Income

  • W-2 from each employer
  • 1099-NEC, 1099-K or your own records for gig and self-employed work
  • 1099-INT, 1099-DIV and 1099-B for bank and investment accounts
  • SSA-1099 for Social Security and 1099-R for retirement withdrawals

Deductions and credits

  • Form 1098 for mortgage interest, and your property tax bill
  • Form 1098-T for college tuition
  • Your child care provider’s name, address and tax ID number
  • Form 1095-A if you had Marketplace health insurance
  • Records of any estimated tax payments you made for 2026

Not sure whether a form matters? Upload a photo in the client portal and we will tell you. You can also start your return now and get a checklist made for your situation.

General information for the tax year shown. Your situation may be different, so ask us before you act on it.
