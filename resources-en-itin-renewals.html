ITIN renewals: is your number still valid?

An ITIN that has not been used on a federal tax return for three years in a row expires. Here is how to check and renew it.

By Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory · Published September 8, 2026

An Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) is for people who need to file U.S. taxes but cannot get a Social Security number.

When an ITIN expires

An ITIN expires if it is not used on a federal tax return at least once in three consecutive tax years. It expires on December 31 of the third year. All ITINs issued before 2013 also expired by the end of 2020 unless they were renewed.

Why it matters

If you file with an expired ITIN, the IRS can delay your refund and hold back some credits until the number is renewed.

How to renew

  • Fill in Form W-7 and check the box to renew an existing ITIN
  • Include original identity documents or copies certified by the agency that issued them; a valid passport works on its own
  • Renew for each family member who needs an ITIN on the return. A spouse or dependent does not always need to renew, and we check this for you

Renew before you file so your refund is not delayed. We review your documents and send the application for you. See our services.
