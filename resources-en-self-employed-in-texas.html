Self-employed in Texas: what you owe and when

Texas has no state income tax, but self-employed people still owe federal income tax and self-employment tax. Here is how it works for 2026.

By Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory · Published September 22, 2026 · Tax year 2026

Rideshare drivers, nail technicians, contractors and freelancers in Texas do not file a state income tax return. The federal return is where the work is.

Self-employment tax

If your net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more, you owe self-employment tax for Social Security and Medicare on top of income tax. The rate is 15.3% on most net earnings, and you deduct half of it on your return.

Quarterly estimated payments

No employer withholds tax for you, so the IRS expects payments during the year. For tax year 2026 the due dates are:

  • April 15, 2026
  • June 15, 2026
  • September 15, 2026
  • January 15, 2027

Records that lower your tax

  • A mileage log with the date, place and reason for each work trip
  • Receipts for supplies, tools, your phone and software
  • Your 1099-NEC and 1099-K forms, plus your own income records

Texas franchise tax

Sole proprietors are not subject to the Texas franchise tax, but an LLC usually is, even when it owes no tax. Ask us whether your business has to file a report.
