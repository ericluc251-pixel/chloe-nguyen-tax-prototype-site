Filed an extension? Your 2025 return is due October 15, 2026

An extension gave you more time to file, not more time to pay. Here is what to do before October 15.

By Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory · Published October 1, 2026 · Tax year 2025

If you filed Form 4868 by April 15, 2026, your 2025 individual return is due October 15, 2026.

More time to file, not to pay

The extension did not extend the time to pay. If you still owe 2025 tax, interest has been adding up since April 15, and a late-payment penalty of 0.5% of the unpaid tax per month usually applies.

If you miss October 15

The penalty for filing late is usually 5% of the unpaid tax for each month or part of a month, up to 25%. If you are owed a refund there is no late-filing penalty, but you generally have three years from the original due date to claim it.

What to do this week

  • Upload any missing forms to the client portal
  • Review and sign your Form 8879 as soon as it is ready
  • Pay any balance due through IRS Direct Pay or your IRS online account

Live in an area with a federally declared disaster? The IRS sometimes gives more time, so check before you rely on it. Our help center lists the other key dates.
