Privacy and security

How Chloe Nguyen Tax Advisory collects, uses, stores and protects your information.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to prepare your return and serve you:

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, mailing address and preferred language.
  • Tax information: income forms (W-2, 1099, K-1), deductions, dependents and prior-year returns that you share with us.
  • The last 4 digits of Social Security numbers, ITINs, EINs and bank accounts, so we can match your records.
  • Messages and appointments you send through our website or portal.
  • How you found us: the campaign link or referral code you used, if any.

What we never store

  • Full Social Security numbers, ITINs or EINs. Our records keep the last 4 digits at most, and our forms warn you and block saving a full number. Full numbers are entered only in the IRS-authorized tax software we use to file your return.
  • Full bank account, routing or card numbers. We keep the last 4 digits at most. Payments go through a payment provider, so card numbers never enter our systems.
  • Passwords to your bank, payroll or IRS accounts. We will never ask for them.

We do not sell your information. We do not use or share your tax return information for anything other than preparing your return, unless the law allows it or you give written consent (Internal Revenue Code section 7216).

Where your data lives

  • Staff records (clients, returns, document lists, invoices) are kept in the firm's Microsoft 365 account, in an Excel workbook in OneDrive. Microsoft encrypts stored files, and our staff sign in with multi-factor authentication. Clients never get access to that workbook.
  • The client portal on this website is a demonstration. It runs on sample data stored in your own browser. Please do not enter real tax information in it.
  • Requests you send through the website are reviewed by our staff before anything is added to your file.
  • This website has no database server of its own. What you type stays in your browser until you send it.

How we use AI

We may use AI tools to help read documents and draft explanations. The rules:

  • Before any text goes to an AI service, Social Security numbers, ITINs, EINs and bank and card numbers are masked.
  • A preparer reviews every AI result. AI never files a return or sends you a message on its own.
  • Before we connect a real AI service, we will choose a provider whose terms limit the use of your data to serving us and do not allow training on it.
  • Today the AI features on this website are simulated and send nothing outside your browser.

Analytics

  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • When you arrive through one of our campaign links, we note the campaign (source, medium and campaign name) and any referral code in your browser's storage, and attach it to your request if you contact us. This tells us which outreach works.
  • If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we record nothing.

How long we keep records

  • Returns and supporting records: at least 3 years after the return is filed, as federal law requires of paid preparers, and normally no longer than 7 years.
  • Inquiries from people who do not become clients are deleted when we no longer need them.
  • Records under a legal hold (for example, an IRS examination) are kept until the hold ends.
  • When the period ends, we securely delete or destroy the records and keep a log of the disposal.

Your choices

  • Marketing messages: use the unsubscribe or STOP instructions in any message, or tell us. We stop marketing but still send messages about your return.
  • Consent you gave: you can withdraw consent at any time, for example to publish your testimonial or to use your information for a purpose you approved. Use the portal or contact us.
  • Your records: ask for a copy of your documents and returns, or ask us to correct information.
  • Language: we explain this notice in English, Vietnamese or Spanish.

How we protect your information

  • A written information security plan (WISP) that follows IRS Publication 4557 (Safeguarding Taxpayer Data) and Publication 5708, and the FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314), which applies to tax preparers under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.
  • A named person responsible for the security program, who reviews risks at least once a year.
  • Multi-factor authentication on staff accounts and an automatic lock in our staff app.
  • Access only for the staff who work on your file, and a log of every change.
  • Staff training, encrypted connections and storage, and secure disposal of old records.
  • An incident response plan. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you, and the IRS, the FTC and the Texas Attorney General when the law requires.
  • We do not claim any third-party security certification.

Guides we follow: IRS Publication 4557 · IRS Publication 5708 · FTC Safeguards Rule
